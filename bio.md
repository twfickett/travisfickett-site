Travis Fickett is a writer and showrunner with twenty-five years of
experience across television, film, comics, and criticism. He
co-created and co-ran *12 Monkeys* for Syfy, and has written for
*Magic: The Gathering*, *Star Trek: Picard*, and *MacGyver*, among others.

Prior to working as a professional television writer, Travis worked as a film executive for Newmarket Films, acquiring *Donnie Darko*, and as a TV critic for IGN, where he helped launch the *Channel Surfing* podcast.

He is represented by CAA and Industry Entertainment.
