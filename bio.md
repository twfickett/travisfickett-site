Travis Fickett is a writer and showrunner with twenty-five years of
experience across television, film, and comics. He
co-created and showran *12 Monkeys* for Syfy, and has written for
*Magic: The Gathering*, *Star Trek: Picard*, and *MacGyver*.

He operates through his production company, Elliesam, Inc., and is
represented by CAA and Industry Entertainment.
