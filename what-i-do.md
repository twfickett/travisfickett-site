## Writing for Television
I co-created and was the showrunner on Syfy’s *12 Monkeys*, and I’ve written and produced on shows including *Terra Nova* for Fox, The CW’s *Nikita*, CBS’s *MacGyver* and *Star Trek: Picard* for Paramount+.

Coming next: *Magic: The Gathering*, an adult animated series for Netflix.

I’ve also run shows in development, including Disney’s *Witch Mountain*, a *Castlevania* spin-off for Netflix, and an animated series for Riot Games.

## Writing for Film
I’ve developed features for Sony Pictures, Warner Bros., and producers like Davis Entertainment.

I started out as an executive for Newmarket Films, an independent production company that made *Memento* (which I did not work on), acquired *Donnie Darko* (which I did work on), and financed and co-produced numerous films during my tenure. That seat on the other side of the development table has proven invaluable in my career.

## Writing for Comics
I co-wrote *Star Trek: Hive* for IDW, a four-part series later collected as a trade paperback, and *Witch* for Kickstart Comics, an original graphic novel.

I love comics. I love writing comics. I am aiming to do more in that space.

## Mentoring
It took me years to break into the business. And lots of false starts. I’ve made all of the mistakes and I like to keep others from making them. I like to help real talent find their own way into this crazy business. I don’t know the direct path or the absolute right answer, but I can spot the routes best not taken and how to prepare yourself best for when opportunities arise.

## Consulting
I don’t have to be on staff to help you and your project. I’ve been brought in at every stage of a production to offer advice, notes, creative changes and fixes. Sometimes another pair of (very experienced) eyes is all it takes to get a project over the finish line.
