Bio

In brief

Travis Fickett is a writer and showrunner with twenty-five years of experience across television, film, comics, and criticism. He co-created and co-ran 12 Monkeys for Syfy, and has written for Star Trek: Picard, Magic: The Gathering, MacGyver, among others. He is represented by CAA and Industry Entertainment.
