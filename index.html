Bio

Longer version

Travis Fickett is a writer and showrunner with twenty-five years of experience across television, film, comics, and audio drama. He co-created and showran 12 Monkeys for Syfy, and has written for Castlevania, Star Trek: Picard, Magic: The Gathering, and MacGyver. He operates through his production company, Elliesam, Inc., and is represented by CAA and Industry Entertainment.
